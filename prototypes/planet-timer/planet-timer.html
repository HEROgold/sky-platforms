Fleeting planet timer, logic prototype

How should a fleeting planet's timer behave so it feels like tension, not punishment, and what happens to a platform still on the ground when time runs out?

Throwaway prototype. Change the rules, then play the situations below. Thud facts built in: landing takes 20 s, take-off takes 25 s (5 s countdown, 20 s climb), take-off needs a full damper charge of 2 MJ per floor tile, and a platform on a planet that disappears is lost. Travelling to a planet takes 60 s. Platforms start with 5 MW of power for charging.
